## Final prompt — twilight background

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production-ready 2D indie RPG environment background for an interactive Florida cypress-tree world, not a website mockup.
Primary request: an original, professionally art-directed Florida swamp and scrub at gentle twilight, cozy, mature, inviting and slightly mysterious. Painterly gouache with delicate etched botanical detail and a subtle paper grain, beautifully composed shapes and atmospheric depth; high-quality commercial indie game background.
Composition: wide landscape, layers of tall distant bald cypress silhouettes and hanging Spanish moss framing the far left and right; low saw palmetto and reeds at the bottom edges; calm swamp water across the lower third. Keep the middle fifty percent visually open with distant mist and water because a separate gigantic interactive tree trunk will be rendered over it by software. Do not paint a central tree. No characters, houses or mountains. Gentle warm crescent moon in the upper right, dusk reflection across quiet water, tiny distant warm firefly points.
Palette: restrained slate-lavender sky #696779, muted dusty-rose horizon #B68C91, deep evergreen silhouettes #28362D and #344B40, soft sage #9FAB83, pale warm brass moonlight #D8BC81. Natural, subtle light; NOT a saturated magenta/orange sunset. Crisp intentional silhouettes against luminous soft haze, sophisticated contrast, no harsh black outlines. Leave the lowest edge dark and softly textured. No readable text, no symbols or lettering, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no frame. Fully painted edge-to-edge opaque image.

## Final prompt — bark

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production 2D game texture for the bark of an enormous Florida bald cypress, designed to tile seamlessly top-to-bottom (vertical repeat only).
Primary request: beautifully hand-painted, tactile, mature cypress bark with fine fibrous vertical ridges, long narrow irregular plates, tiny knots and warm weathered grain. Painterly gouache realism with restrained etched detail, the polish of a commercially successful atmospheric indie RPG. It must be unmistakably organic bark, not stone bricks, not wooden planks.
Composition: close-up front-facing orthographic bark surface fills the entire image, landscape format. Flat diffuse neutral lighting; absolutely seamless matching top and bottom edges. No perspective, no outer tree silhouette, no sky, no branches, no large focal knot, no central hotspot, no strong shadows. Low-contrast fine organic detail suitable as a canvas behind visitors' drawings.
Palette: warm muted ochre-taupe midtones #B58D62 with soft highlights #C5A77D and crevice accents #5B4835, subtly desaturated and gently luminous. No green moss patches. Keep total contrast controlled so both dark brown carvings and softly glowing magical strokes will read over the texture. No words, marks, runes, logos, watermark or UI. Every edge fully opaque.

## Final prompt — transparent foreground

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent foreground vegetation layer for the same atmospheric Florida swamp indie RPG environment. Image 1 is ONLY a style and palette reference; generate a NEW isolated asset, do not edit or reproduce the background.
Primary request: a refined hand-painted frame of saw palmetto fans, fine reeds and hanging Spanish moss, believable Florida plants, soft etched gouache detail and restrained grain matching Image 1. Composition wide landscape. Plants cluster in the lower left and lower right corners, sparse strands of Spanish moss descending from the top corners. The central sixty percent of the image and most of the top must be completely empty TRANSPARENT negative space for an interactive tree and UI. Bottom center should remain empty too. Thin irregular organic silhouette, not a solid border; dark evergreen and sage with tiny subdued brass edge highlights.
Constraints: genuine transparent background/alpha, no sky, no water, no scenery behind the plants, no central tree, no human, no animals, no floating words, no logos, no watermark, no frame. Restrained and mature, not cartoonish, never bright purple. All plants contained within the canvas with believable detail at the visible edges.

The background PNG was used solely as a palette/style reference for the foreground. No reference-site artwork was used as an edit target or copied.
